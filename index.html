Actual balances

A snapshot is the total balance you observed at a particular instant.

Snapshots include cashflows at the exact same time. Duplicate snapshot times are rejected. Tab saves a cell; Enter saves and moves to the next row; Escape restores the saved cell.

New balances need a date and amount. Zero is a valid balance.

Clear rules, consistent dates. Monthly day 31 and yearly leap-day recurrences clamp to the last valid day. Any transactions occurring or recurring before today's current actual balance are ignored.
